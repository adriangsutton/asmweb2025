Adrian is in the middle of work to combine his early three duo piano pieces into a new revised work for two pianos entitled Double Piano Triptych. The three pieces are: Bicycle Dance In Lanes Of Leaves and Light, Old Man Explains a Lifelong Dilemma, and a two-piano version of Adrian's epic Aerobatics over Lake Wanaka for six pianos, originally performed by Piano Circus.

Plans for performance are in the pipeline soon!
