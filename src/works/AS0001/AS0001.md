The Double Piano Triptych, one of Adrian's earliest works, is a suite of three pieces, written between 1992-1998, inspired originally by the multi-piano group Piano Circus. They continue to perform the six-piano version of <i>Aerobatics Over Lake Wanaka</i> to this day.

These pieces have now been reimagined and refactored for a two-piano, two-player version for World Premier in the autumn.

The three vignettes are intended to be performed as a complete suite, but can also be performed as standalone works in their own right, each depicting a different scene:

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-A">Bicycle Dance in Lanes of Leaves and Light</a></b>

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-B">Old Man Explains a Lifelong Dilemma</a></b>

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-C">Aerobatics Over Lake Wanaka</a></b>

The final movement also exists as a <a href="./works/AS0001-D">six-piano version</a>
