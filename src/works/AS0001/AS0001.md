The Double Piano Triptych, one of Adrian's earliest works, is a suite of three pieces, written over 1992-1998, inspired originally by the multi-piano group Piano Circus. 

These pieces have now been reimagined and refactored for a two-piano, two-player version for World Premier in the autumn.  The suite works as a series of three vignettes, each performable as standalone works in their own right.

It consists of three movements, each depicting a different scene:

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-A">Bicycle Dance in Lanes of Leaves and Light</a></b>

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-B">Old Man Explains a Lifelong Dilemma</a></b>

<b><a href="./works/AS0001-C">Aerobatics Over Lake Wanaka</a></b>
