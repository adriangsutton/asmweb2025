This piece is the first movement of the <a href="./works/AS0001">Double Piano Triptych</a>.

A joyful and energetic piece, drawing material from one of Adrian’s very earliest works, it imagines a “ballet” between two cyclists, careering up and down a cycle path in the autumn woods, glittering with dappled sunbeams.
